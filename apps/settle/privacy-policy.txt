PRIVACY POLICY — SETTLE

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile app "Settle" (Google Play and App Store name "Settle: Acid Reflux Tracker", package name com.appkickstarter.settle), called "the App" below.

The App is developed and published by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer (developer name on Google Play: "Louis Duboscq"; website: appkickstarter.com). Louis Duboscq is the data controller for the data described in this policy.

Contact: hey@appkickstarter.com


1. SUMMARY

- Your health logs stay on your phone: dinners and meals, symptoms, how your nights and mornings went, notes and settings are never sent to us or to anyone else.
- There is no account, no advertising, no advertising ID and no sale of data.
- The App only sends data in the cases listed in section 3: purchases (when Premium is offered), the ideas & bugs board or cancellation survey (only what you choose to write), and crash reports and anonymous usage statistics (on by default, you can turn them off at any time).
- The App does not connect to Apple Health or Health Connect and does not read any health data from other apps.


2. DATA STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The following data is stored in the App's local database on your phone. It never leaves your device, we cannot access it, and it is not backed up to our servers:

- Your dinners and meals: time, size, tags you pick (for example fatty, spicy, coffee, alcohol).
- Your symptoms: night reflux, the symptoms you wake up with (for example heartburn, jaw clenching, headache, back pain), your morning check-ins and bedtime.
- The symptoms you choose during onboarding.
- Your history, the insights the App computes from it on your phone, and anything you edit or add by hand.
- Your settings and reminders.

If you use the Premium report or export, the file is generated on your phone. It is only saved or shared when you choose to do so, through the app you pick.

Deleting the App deletes all of this data from your phone. The App has no cloud backup in this version.


3. DATA THAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE

a) Purchases — when Premium is offered
Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. To check your Premium status, the App uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (product, dates, renewal status). Purpose: unlocking Premium and restoring purchases. No advertising identifier is used.

b) Ideas & bugs board — only what you post
If you post or vote on the Ideas & bugs board, the App sends the text you write, your votes, the App's anonymous random identifier (see f below), the App version, your platform and language, and your email address only if you choose to enter it (it is never shown publicly and only used to reply to you). Requests can be shown to other users of the App. They are stored with Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), and their text may be translated into other languages by an AI service (OpenAI) so other users can read them: a server function of ours, hosted with Supabase, sends only the text of the request to OpenAI, has it translated and keeps the translation on the board. Do not include personal or health information in your posts.

c) Cancellation survey — only what you answer
If you cancel your subscription from inside the App, you can tell us why. The reason you pick, the optional comment you write, the App, your platform, store and language, and the App's anonymous random identifier (see f below) are stored with Supabase to improve the App.

d) Usage statistics — anonymous, on by default, you can turn them off
Usage statistics and crash reports are on by default, without a question at launch: you can turn them off at any time in Settings > What is sent. Unless you turn them off, the App sends to PostHog (PostHog Inc., EU cloud, servers in the European Union): the steps you go through (for example the onboarding screens, the Premium screen viewed or closed and the plan chosen, "first dinner logged" — never a time, a meal, a symptom or anything about your health), when the App is opened or put in the background, a random identifier created on your phone (replaced after 13 months at most), the App version, your phone model and operating system, language and time zone, and the type of network (Wi-Fi or mobile, and the carrier name). PostHog also works out your country from your internet address when the statistics arrive; only the country is kept, not the address, region or city. There is no account, no profile and no screen recording, and this data is not linked to your name or email. Purpose: understanding which features and screens help, to improve the App. Retention: up to 1 year.

We do not use any advertising SDK, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal data with data brokers or advertisers.

e) Crash reports — on by default, you can turn them off
As for usage statistics (see d above), you can turn crash reports off at any time in Settings > What is sent. Unless you turn them off, the App sends anonymous technical data to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., sentry.io), our crash reporting provider: the error and where it happened, phone model, operating system and App version, the screens used just before the problem (their names only, never their content), how fast the App starts, when it opens and closes, the App's anonymous random identifier (see f below) and whether you have Premium. It never includes your meals, symptoms, sleep times, notes or anything about your health. Purpose: finding and fixing bugs. Retention: up to 90 days.

f) The App's anonymous identifier
When you first open the App, it creates a random identifier (a UUID) for this App only. It is not your name, your email, your phone's advertising ID or any identifier shared with other apps. Your phone's secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Block Store) can keep it so that it survives a reinstall. The App sends it to Sentry (crash reports), RevenueCat (purchases) and Supabase (ideas & bugs board, cancellation survey), as described above; it is never sent to PostHog. Purpose: recognising the same installation across these services to fix bugs, restore purchases and answer you.


4. DEVICE PERMISSIONS

- Notifications: for the reminders you turn on (for example the evening dinner reminder and the morning check-in). Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no data is sent.
- Internet: only for the cases in section 3.
The App does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, camera or photos.


5. LEGAL BASES (GDPR)

- Data stored on your device (meals, symptoms, notes): not processed by us.
- Purchases: performance of the contract (your subscription).
- Ideas & bugs board, cancellation survey: your consent when you choose to send them, and our legitimate interest in improving the App.
- Crash reports: our legitimate interest in finding and fixing bugs (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning them off in the App.
- Usage statistics: anonymous measurement of how the App is used, limited to what is needed to improve it, which the French data protection authority allows without prior consent (CNIL guidelines on audience measurement: anonymous statistics for this App only, no profile, identifier replaced after 13 months at most, kept up to 1 year); legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the App (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning them off in the App.

Data sent to our providers may be processed outside the European Union (in particular in the United States), under the safeguards those providers offer (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).


6. RETENTION AND YOUR RIGHTS

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or the App. Crash reports are kept up to 90 days, usage statistics up to 1 year. Board posts and survey answers are kept as long as the board or the App exists, unless you ask us to delete them.

You can ask to access, correct or delete the data described in section 3 (for example your board posts or the email you gave), or object to its processing, by writing to hey@appkickstarter.com. As we have no account and only anonymous identifiers, tell us the text of your post or the email you used so we can find it.

How to delete your data, step by step (on your phone, and on our servers: board posts and survey answers, usage statistics, crash reports): https://appkickstarter.com/apps/settle/delete-data.html . We act within 30 days and confirm by email. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France: the CNIL).


7. CHILDREN

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The App never asks for your name, age or any identity detail.


8. NOT A MEDICAL APP

The App is a personal diary and information tool. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose or treat any condition, and it does not replace advice from a doctor. The patterns it shows are personal observations from your own logs, not proof of cause. Always follow the guidance of your healthcare professional, and talk to them about persistent or worrying symptoms.


9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy, for example when the App starts sending something new. The date at the top shows the latest version, and important changes are announced in the App.
