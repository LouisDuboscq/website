Delete your Settle data

This page is for Settle ("Settle: Acid Reflux Tracker" on the App Store and Google Play, package com.appkickstarter.settle), developed by Louis Duboscq.

Settle has no account. Your meals, nights, symptoms, notes and settings are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us, so there is nothing of them to delete on our side.

On your phone (immediate)

  • iPhone: touch and hold the Settle icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App.
  • Android: long-press the Settle icon and tap Uninstall. To keep the app but erase its data: Settings > Apps > Settle > Storage > Clear storage.

All your logs are erased from the phone. They cannot be recovered, as Settle has no cloud backup.

On our servers (on request)

A few things can leave your phone: what you post on the Ideas board or write in the cancellation survey (Supabase), anonymous usage statistics (PostHog, EU), crash reports (Sentry) and your purchase history (RevenueCat). None of them contains your meals or symptoms. To have them deleted, write to hey@appkickstarter.com with the subject "Delete my Settle data", and include the text of your post or the email address you typed, if any, so we can find it. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your Ideas board posts, votes and survey answers, and the email address you may have given.
  • The anonymous identifiers of your installation, with the usage statistics, crash reports and purchase records tied to them.

Without the app, we cannot link anonymous statistics or crash reports to you, so tell us your installation identifier only if you have it. Statistics are in any case kept up to 1 year and crash reports up to 90 days, then erased automatically. Purchases stay in your App Store or Google Play account, which only the store can delete.
