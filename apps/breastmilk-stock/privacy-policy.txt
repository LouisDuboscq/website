PRIVACY POLICY — BREASTMILK STOCK

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile app "Breastmilk Stock" (Google Play and App Store name "Breastmilk Stock: Pump & Stash", package name com.appkickstarter.breastmilkstock), called "the App" below.

The App is developed and published by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer (developer name on Google Play: "Louis Duboscq"; website: appkickstarter.com). Louis Duboscq is the data controller for the data described in this policy.

Contact: hey@appkickstarter.com


1. SUMMARY

- Your milk stock stays on your phone: containers, volumes, dates, storage places, daycare plan, feeds and settings are never sent to us or to anyone else, unless you turn on the optional cloud backup (section 3 f).
- An account is optional: you only create one, with your email address, to turn on the backup. There is no advertising, no advertising ID and no sale of data.
- The App only sends data in the cases listed in section 3: adding containers by voice with AI, purchases (when Premium is offered), the ideas & bugs board or cancellation survey (only what you choose to write), crash reports and anonymous usage statistics only if you agree to them, and your cloud backup only if you turn it on.


2. DATA STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The following data is stored in the App's local database on your phone. Unless you turn on the cloud backup (section 3 f), it never leaves your device, we cannot access it, and it is not backed up to our servers:

- Your milk containers: volume, pumping date and time, storage place (fridge, freezer, daycare), use-by date, labels, and what was drunk or thrown away.
- Your storage rules (fridge, freezer and thawed durations), which you choose yourself.
- Your daycare and care schedule, feeding rhythm and the first names you give to your children, if any.
- Your settings, alerts and trophies.

Images you create in the Share feature are generated on your phone. They are only saved to your photos or shared when you choose to do so, through the sharing app you pick.

Deleting the App deletes all of this data from your phone (and only from your phone: a cloud backup, if you made one, stays until you delete it, see section 6).


3. DATA THAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE

a) Adding containers by voice (AI) — only when you use it
Speech is turned into text on your phone; the audio is never sent. When you dictate new containers (for example "three bags of 90 ml from this morning in the freezer"), that text and today's date are sent to our own server, which asks an AI service (OpenAI) to turn it into containers. Nothing else from your stock is sent, and the text is not stored by us. Avoid saying information you would not want to send. Dictating stock movements (drunk, thrown away, taken to daycare) is understood on your phone and sends nothing.

b) Purchases — when Premium is offered
Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. To check your Premium status, the App uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (product, dates, renewal status). Purpose: unlocking Premium and restoring purchases. If you installed the App from an Apple Ads advertisement (App Store search ads), RevenueCat also receives from Apple which ad campaign and keyword led to the install (Apple's standard attribution data, iOS only). No advertising identifier is used and nothing is used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Purpose: measuring our own ads.

c) Ideas & bugs board — only what you post
If you post or vote on the Ideas & bugs board, the App sends the text you write, your votes, an anonymous random device identifier, the App version, your platform and language, and your email address only if you choose to enter it (it is never shown publicly and only used to reply to you). Requests can be shown to other users of the App. They are stored with Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), and their text may be translated into other languages by an AI service (OpenAI, through our own server) so other users can read them. Do not include personal or health information in your posts.

d) Cancellation survey — only what you answer
If you cancel your subscription from inside the App, you can tell us why. The reason you pick, the optional comment you write, the App, your platform, store and language are stored anonymously with Supabase to improve the App.

e) Usage statistics — only if you agree
The App asks once whether you agree to share anonymous usage statistics; you can change your answer at any time in Settings > What is sent. Until you answer, these events wait on your phone; if you say no, they are deleted and nothing is sent. If you say yes, the App sends to PostHog (PostHog Inc., EU cloud, servers in the European Union): the steps you go through (for example the welcome video watched, the Premium screen viewed or closed and the plan chosen, "milk added" with fridge or freezer — never a volume, a date, a note or anything about your baby), when the App is opened or put in the background, a random identifier created on your phone, the App version, your phone model and operating system, language and time zone, the type of network (Wi-Fi or mobile, and the carrier name). PostHog also works out your country from your internet address when the statistics arrive; only the country is kept, not the address, region or city. There is no account, no profile and no screen recording, and this data is not linked to your name or email. Purpose: understanding which features and screens help, to improve the App. Retention: up to 1 year.

We do not use any advertising SDK, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal data with data brokers or advertisers.


f) Crash reports — only if you agree
The App asks once whether crash reports may be sent; you can change your answer at any time in Settings > What is sent. If you say yes, and only then, the App sends anonymous technical data to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., sentry.io), our crash reporting provider: the error and where it happened, phone model, operating system and App version, the screens used just before the problem (their names only, never their content), how fast the App starts, and when it opens and closes. It never includes your stock, volumes, dates, notes, places or your baby's name. Purpose: finding and fixing bugs. Retention: up to 90 days.

g) Cloud backup and account — only if you turn it on
The backup is optional and off by default. If you turn it on (Settings > Backup), you create an account with your email address: we email you a 6-digit code to sign in, there is no password. The App then sends, after each change and when it starts: your containers (volume, dates, storage place, use-by date, notes, numbers), what was drunk or thrown away and by which child, the first names of your children, your storage rules, alert, daycare and care schedule settings, your trophies and date formats, and the kind of device ("Android" or "iPhone"). It never sends your baby's age, your location or your contacts. This data and your email address are stored with Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), our hosting provider, linked to your account; only you can read your backup (each account can only read and write its own backup, also when calling the server directly). Purpose: restoring your data on a new phone, after reinstalling the App, or from iPhone to Android. We do not use it for anything else and never share it. You can delete your backup and your account at any time (section 6).


4. DEVICE PERMISSIONS

- Microphone and speech recognition: to dictate containers and stock movements. The audio stays on your phone (see section 3 a for the text of new containers).
- Photos: to save the images you create in the Share feature, when you ask.
- Notifications: for the use-by and daycare alerts you turn on. Alerts are scheduled on your phone; no data is sent.
- Internet: only for the cases in section 3.


5. LEGAL BASES (GDPR)

- Data stored on your device: not processed by us.
- Adding containers by voice: your request (you start the dictation).
- Purchases: performance of the contract (your subscription).
- Cloud backup and account: performance of the service you ask for when you turn it on, and your explicit consent for the health-related data it contains (the milk your baby drank).
- Ideas & bugs board, cancellation survey: your consent when you choose to send them, and our legitimate interest in improving the App.
- Crash reports and usage statistics: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings > What is sent.

Data sent to our providers may be processed outside the European Union (in particular in the United States), under the safeguards those providers offer (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).


6. RETENTION AND YOUR RIGHTS

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or the App. Dictated text is not kept by us after it is turned into containers. Crash reports are kept up to 90 days, usage statistics up to 1 year. Board posts and survey answers are kept as long as the board or the App exists, unless you ask us to delete them. Your cloud backup and your account are kept until you delete them; only the latest backup is kept (each new one replaces the previous one).

Deleting your backup and your account: in the App, Settings > Backup > Delete my backup. This deletes your backup and your account (email address) from our servers right away; the data on your phone stays. If you no longer have the App, follow https://appkickstarter.com/apps/breastmilk-stock/delete-account.html or write to hey@appkickstarter.com from the email address of your account: we delete them within 30 days. Signing out (Settings > Backup > Sign out) only stops the backups on that phone.

You can ask to access, correct or delete the data described in section 3 (for example your board posts or the email you gave), or object to its processing, by writing to hey@appkickstarter.com. Without an account we only have anonymous identifiers, so tell us the text of your post or the email you used so we can find it; with a backup account, write from its email address. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France: the CNIL).


7. CHILDREN

The App is used by parents. It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The App never asks for your baby's age.


8. NOT A MEDICAL APP

The App is a stock tracking tool. It does not give storage durations, quantities or medical advice: you choose your own storage rules. Always follow the guidance of your healthcare professional and your daycare.


9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy, for example when the App starts sending something new. The date at the top shows the latest version, and important changes are announced in the App.
