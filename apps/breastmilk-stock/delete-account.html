Delete your Breastmilk Stock account and backup

This page is for Breastmilk Stock ("Breastmilk Stock: Pump & Stash" on the App Store and Google Play, package com.appkickstarter.breastmilkstock), developed by Louis Duboscq.

An account only exists if you turned on the optional cloud backup (Settings > Backup). Without it, everything stays on your phone and there is nothing to delete on our side.

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Breastmilk Stock and go to Settings > Backup.
  2. Tap Delete my backup, then confirm.

Your backup and your account are deleted from our servers right away.

Without the app

Write to hey@appkickstarter.com from the email address of your account, with the subject "Delete my Breastmilk Stock account". We delete your account and your backup within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address and account identifier.
  • Your backup: containers, what was drunk or thrown away, your children's first names, your storage rules and settings.

Nothing is kept after deletion (no copy, no archive). The data on your phone is not touched: delete the app to remove it. Posts on the in-app Ideas & bugs board are anonymous and not linked to your account; to remove one, write to us with its text.
