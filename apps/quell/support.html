Quell: Notification Blocker — Support

Quell: Notification Blocker (Android, package com.appkickstarter.quell) is developed by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: hey@appkickstarter.com, or the Ideas tab in the app.

Notifications do not show up

Quell only sees what your apps put in their notifications. Check that:

  • notification access is on for Quell (Android settings > Notifications > Device & app notifications);
  • battery optimization does not stop Quell (Android settings > Apps > Quell > Battery > Unrestricted);
  • the app is not set to "Allow" if you expected it to be muted or summarized.

Notifications received before Quell had access cannot be recovered.

Premium

Quell Premium (yearly subscription or lifetime purchase) is billed by Google Play. To cancel a subscription, open Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. To get Premium back on a new phone, tap "Restore purchases" in the app.

Your data

The notifications Quell reads never leave your phone; there is no account. Removing notification access in Android settings or deleting the app stops and deletes everything. Details: Privacy policy.
