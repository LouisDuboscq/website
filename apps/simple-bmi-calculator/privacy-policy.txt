PRIVACY POLICY — SIMPLE BMI CALCULATOR

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app "Simple BMI Calculator" (Google Play listing "Simple BMI Calculator", French listing "Calculateur d'IMC Simple", package name com.appkickstarter.simplebmicalculator), called "the App" below.

The App is developed and published by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer (developer name on Google Play: "Louis Duboscq"; website: appkickstarter.com). Louis Duboscq is the data controller for the data described in this policy.

Contact: hey@appkickstarter.com


1. SUMMARY

- The values you enter (such as height and weight) are used on your phone to calculate your BMI. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
- There is no account.
- The App shows ads from Google AdMob, which receives your device's advertising ID, never the values you enter (section 3).


2. DATA STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The values you enter and your settings are processed and, when the App keeps them, stored on your phone only. We cannot access them. Deleting the App deletes this data.


3. DATA THAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE: ADVERTISING

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To request, show and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends to Google your device's advertising ID (or app set ID), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, your phone model, operating system, language and App version, and ad interactions (which ads were shown and clicked). Ad requests never contain your height, weight, BMI or anything you enter. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads are personalized only with your consent. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not use analytics or crash reporting services, we do not sell your data and we do not share it with data brokers.


4. DEVICE PERMISSIONS

- Internet and advertising ID: only for ads (section 3).


5. LEGAL BASES (GDPR)

- Data on your device: not processed by us.
- Ads: your consent for personalized ads and for storing or accessing information on your device; Google's own legal bases for non-personalized ads.

Data sent to Google may be processed outside the European Union (in particular in the United States), under the safeguards Google offers (such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).


6. RETENTION AND YOUR RIGHTS

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or the App. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own policies. As we keep no data about you, we have nothing to access or delete on our side; for advertising data, use your Android ad settings or Google's tools (https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy). Questions: hey@appkickstarter.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France: the CNIL).


7. CHILDREN

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires) and we do not knowingly collect data from children.


8. NOT MEDICAL ADVICE

BMI is a general indicator. The App does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Consult a healthcare professional for health decisions.


9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy, for example when the App starts sending something new. The date at the top shows the latest version.
