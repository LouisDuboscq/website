Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Simple BMI Calculator (“the App”) handles data and clarifies its scope and limitations.

The App is designed with a strict privacy-by-design approach and does not collect any user data.

No Data Collection – Ever

The App does not collect, process, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data.

Specifically:

  • No personal information
  • No health data collection
  • No weight, height, age, or measurement storage outside the device
  • No analytics
  • No tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reporting
  • No cookies
  • No third-party SDKs collecting data

Zero data is collected. Zero data leaves the device.

No Account, No Identification

The App does not require account creation.

As a result:

  • Users are not identifiable
  • No profiles exist
  • No authentication is performed
  • No data can be linked to a person

We do not know who uses the App.

Local-Only Processing

Simple BMI Calculator operates entirely on-device.

  • All calculations (BMI, body fat estimation, BMR, calorie estimation, etc.) are performed locally
  • No input data is transmitted
  • No information is uploaded
  • No synchronization with external servers occurs

Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored App data.

Data Access, Deletion & Legal Requests

Because the App does not collect or store any data externally:

  • We cannot access your data
  • We cannot retrieve, export, or delete data on request
  • We cannot respond to GDPR / CCPA data access or deletion requests, as we do not possess any user data

All data control remains solely with the user via their own device.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not specifically designed for children.

  • The App does not knowingly collect data from children
  • No personal information is processed

As no data collection occurs, no children’s personal data is stored or transmitted.

Informational Disclaimer

Simple BMI Calculator provides general informational calculations only.

  • Results are statistical estimates
  • The App does not provide medical advice
  • The App does not replace professional healthcare consultation

Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.

Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law:

  • The App’s creators assume no responsibility for decisions made based on calculated results
  • No guarantees are made regarding accuracy, completeness, or fitness for a particular purpose

Use of the App is at your own discretion.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected in the App or its store listing.
