Body Measurement Tracker — Support

Body Measurement Tracker (App Store: "Body Measurements: Tape & BMI") is developed by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: hey@appkickstarter.com. You can also use the Ideas tab inside the app to suggest features and report bugs.

Subscription

Premium is billed by the App Store or Google Play. To cancel, open your store subscriptions (iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions; Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions). To get Premium back on a new phone, tap "Restore purchases" in the app.

Your data

Your measurements, photos and health data stay on your phone; there is no account. Deleting the app deletes them. Details: Privacy policy.
