Rebalance — Support

Rebalance (package and bundle id com.appkickstarter.rebalance) is developed by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: hey@appkickstarter.com, or the Ideas tab in the app.

Premium

Premium is billed by the App Store or Google Play. To cancel, open your store subscriptions (iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions; Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions). To get Premium back on a new phone, tap "Restore purchases" in the app.

Your data

Your meals, ratings and measurements stay on your phone; there is no account. Deleting the app deletes them. Details: Privacy policy.

Terms of use

Apple's standard licence agreement (EULA) applies on iOS.
