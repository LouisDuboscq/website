Calories Track — Support

Calories Track (package and bundle id com.appkickstarter.caloriestrack) is developed by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: hey@appkickstarter.com, or the Ideas tab in the app.

Food or barcode not found

Food search and barcode scan use the free Open Food Facts database, which is built by volunteers: a product can be missing or incomplete. You can add the food yourself with a quick add, and add the missing product on openfoodfacts.org to help everyone.

Premium

Premium is billed by the App Store or Google Play. To cancel, open your store subscriptions (iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions; Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions). To get Premium back on a new phone, tap "Restore purchases" in the app.

Your data

Your food log, goal and body data stay on your phone; there is no account. Only the text you search or the barcode you scan is sent to Open Food Facts. Deleting the app deletes your data. Details: Privacy policy.
