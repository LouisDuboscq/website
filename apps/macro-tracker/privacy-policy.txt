PRIVACY POLICY — MACRO TRACKER

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile app "Macro Tracker" (Google Play and App Store name "Macro Tracker: Macro Counter", package name com.appkickstarter.macrotracker), called "the App" below.

The App is developed and published by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer (developer name on Google Play: "Louis Duboscq"; website: appkickstarter.com). Louis Duboscq is the data controller for the data described in this policy.

Contact: hey@appkickstarter.com


1. SUMMARY

- Your food diary, weight, profile and targets stay on your phone. There is no account and no advertising.
- The App only sends data in the cases listed in section 3: a food search or a scanned barcode (to Open Food Facts), purchases (RevenueCat), the Ideas board and cancellation survey (only what you choose to write), crash reports and anonymous usage statistics (on by default, you can turn them off at any time).
- Your weigh-ins read from Apple Health or Health Connect are never sent anywhere.


2. DATA STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The following data is stored in the App's local database on your phone. It never leaves your device and we cannot access it:

- Your profile (age, height, weight, goal, activity) and the calorie and macro targets worked out from it.
- Your food diary: the foods you log, your own foods and recipes, meals, portions, favourites.
- Your weigh-ins and the weight trend.
- Your settings and reminders (scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent).

Camera: when you scan a barcode, the camera image is read on your phone and is never sent or stored; only the barcode number is used (see section 3 a).

Apple Health (iPhone) and Health Connect (Android), read only: if you turn on "Import my weight" in Settings, the App reads your weigh-ins from Apple Health or Health Connect so your trend stays up to date. They are kept on your phone, never sent to us or to anyone else, never used for advertising, and the App never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect.

Deleting the App deletes all of this data from your phone.


3. DATA THAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE

a) Food search and barcode lookup (Open Food Facts) — only when you search or scan
When you search for a food by name or scan a barcode, the text you typed or the scanned barcode number is sent to the Open Food Facts public database (Open Food Facts, a non-profit association, world.openfoodfacts.org) to get the product and its nutrition values. No account, name or advertising identifier is sent with it. Open Food Facts data is open data published under the Open Database License (ODbL).

b) Purchases — when Premium is offered
Subscriptions and purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check your Premium status, the App uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (product, dates, renewal status). Purpose: unlocking Premium and restoring purchases. If you installed the App from an Apple Ads advertisement (App Store search ads), RevenueCat also receives from Apple which ad campaign and keyword led to the install (Apple's standard attribution data, iOS only). No advertising identifier is used and nothing is used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. On Android, if you installed the App from one of our ad links, the App reads once, from Google Play, the campaign name written in that link and sends it to RevenueCat and with the usage statistics below; it is a label of our own link, not an identifier, and it follows the usage statistics switch.

c) Ideas board — only what you post
If you post or vote on the Ideas board, the App sends the text you write, your votes, an anonymous random identifier created on your phone, the App version, your platform and language, and your email address only if you choose to enter it (it is never shown publicly and only used to reply to you). Posts are checked by the developer before other users can see them. They are stored with Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), and their text may be translated into other languages by an AI service (OpenAI): a server function of ours, hosted with Supabase, reads the post, has it translated and keeps the translation on the board. Do not include personal or health information in your posts.

d) Cancellation survey — only what you answer
If you cancel your subscription from inside the App, you can tell us why. The reason you pick, the optional comment you write, the App, your platform, store and language are stored with Supabase to improve the App.

e) Usage statistics — anonymous, on by default, you can turn them off
Usage statistics and crash reports are on by default: you can turn them off at any time in Settings > What is sent. Unless you turn them off, the App sends to PostHog (PostHog Inc., EU cloud, servers in the European Union): the steps you go through (for example the Premium screen viewed or closed and the plan chosen, "food logged"; never the food, a calorie value, a weight or a note), when the App is opened or put in the background, a random identifier created on your phone, the App version, your phone model and operating system, language and time zone, and the type of network. PostHog also works out your country from your internet address when the statistics arrive; only the country is kept, not the address, region or city. There is no account, no profile and no screen recording. Purpose: understanding which features and screens help, to improve the App. Retention: up to 1 year.

f) Crash reports — on by default, you can turn them off
Unless you turn them off (Settings > What is sent), the App sends technical data to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., sentry.io), our crash reporting provider: the error and where it happened, phone model, operating system and App version, the screens used just before the problem (their names only, never their content), how fast the App starts, and a random identifier created on your phone. It never includes your diary, foods, weight or profile. Purpose: finding and fixing bugs. Retention: up to 90 days.

We do not use any advertising SDK, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not sell or share personal data with data brokers or advertisers.


4. DEVICE PERMISSIONS

- Camera: to scan food barcodes. Images stay on your phone (see section 2).
- Apple Health / Health Connect: to read your weigh-ins, only if you turn it on (see section 2).
- Notifications: for the daily reminder and the weekly check-in note, if you turn them on. Scheduled on your phone; no data is sent.
- Internet: only for the cases in section 3.


5. LEGAL BASES (GDPR)

- Data stored on your device: not processed by us.
- Food search and barcode lookup: your request (you start the search or the scan).
- Purchases: performance of the contract (your subscription).
- Ideas board, cancellation survey: your consent when you choose to send them, and our legitimate interest in improving the App.
- Crash reports: our legitimate interest in finding and fixing bugs (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning them off in the App.
- Usage statistics: anonymous measurement of how the App is used, limited to what is needed to improve it; legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the App (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning them off in the App.

Data sent to our providers may be processed outside the European Union (in particular in the United States), under the safeguards those providers offer (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).


6. RETENTION AND YOUR RIGHTS

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or the App. Crash reports are kept up to 90 days, usage statistics up to 1 year. Board posts and survey answers are kept as long as the board or the App exists, unless you ask us to delete them.

You can ask to access, correct or delete the data described in section 3 (for example your board posts or the email you gave), or object to its processing, by writing to hey@appkickstarter.com. Without an account we only have anonymous identifiers, so tell us the text of your post or the email you used so we can find it. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France: the CNIL).


7. CHILDREN

The App is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.


8. NOT A MEDICAL APP

The App gives general wellness estimates (calories, macros, weight trend). It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Consult a doctor before any medical decision, and follow the guidance of your healthcare professional.


9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy, for example when the App starts sending something new. The date at the top shows the latest version, and important changes are announced in the App.
