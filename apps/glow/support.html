Intermittent Fasting Tracker — Support

Intermittent Fasting Tracker (Google Play; App Store: "Intermittent Fasting & Phases", formerly Glow / Gloweee) is developed by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: hey@appkickstarter.com.

Subscription

Premium is billed by the App Store or Google Play. To cancel, open your store subscriptions (iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions; Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions). To get Premium back on a new phone, tap "Restore purchases" on the Premium screen.

Your data

Your fasts and your weight stay on your phone; there is no account. Deleting the app deletes them. Details: Privacy policy.

Health

The app is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before fasting if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes, an eating disorder or any medical condition, or take medication.
