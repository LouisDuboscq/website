PRIVACY POLICY — INTERMITTENT FASTING TRACKER (GLOW)

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile app published as "Intermittent Fasting Tracker" on Google Play (package name com.appkickstarter.glow, formerly "Gloweee") and as "Intermittent Fasting & Phases" on the App Store (bundle ID com.lduboscq.glow.ios), called "the App" below. It applies from version 2.0 of the App.

The App is developed and published by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer (developer name on Google Play and the App Store: "Louis Duboscq"; website: appkickstarter.com). Louis Duboscq is the data controller for the data described in this policy.

Contact: hey@appkickstarter.com


1. SUMMARY

- Your fasts, fasting days, weigh-ins, plan and settings are stored on your phone only. There is no account.
- If you allow it, the App reads your weight from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS), and keeps it on your phone (section 3).
- The App sends anonymous crash reports and anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off in Settings > What is sent, uses a purchase service for Premium, and sends what you choose to post on the Ideas & feedback board (section 4). It shows no ads; if you come from one of our ads on Android, it measures which ad brought you (section 4d).


2. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

The following data is stored in the App on your phone and is never sent to our servers:

- Your fasts: start and end times, goal and plan
- Your fasting days (5:2 and alternate-day plans) marked as done
- Your weigh-ins, entered by hand or imported (section 3)
- Your settings: plan, reminder time, language, appearance

Deleting the App deletes this data.


3. HEALTH DATA (HEALTH CONNECT ON ANDROID, APPLE HEALTH ON IOS)

If you tap "Connect" when the App offers it, or turn on "Import weigh-ins" (Settings > Health Connect / Apple Health) and grant permission, the App reads the following health data:

- Weight (including past weigh-ins, up to one year on the first import)

How this health data is used:
- It is read only to add your weigh-ins (for example from a connected scale) to your weight curve inside the App.
- It is stored only on your device, in the App's local database. It is never sent to a server, never shared with or sold to anyone, and never used for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than showing your own weight curve.
- The App never writes, changes or deletes any data in Health Connect or Apple Health.
- Access is optional. You can turn it off in the App, and remove the permission at any time in Health Connect or in the iOS Health app. Weigh-ins already imported stay in the App until you delete them or the App.

The App's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your fasts and your weight are never included in crash reports or usage statistics.


4. DATA THAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE

a) Crash reports (on by default, can be turned off)
When the App crashes or hits an error, a report with your phone model, operating system, App version and the technical error is sent to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., United States) to find and fix bugs. Reports contain no user identity, no screenshot and none of your fasts or health data. Turn them off in Settings > What is sent.

b) Usage statistics (on by default, can be turned off)
The App sends anonymous events about how it is used (for example: onboarding step viewed, fast started or ended with its plan and a rounded length in hours, plan chosen, paywall viewed), with a random identifier, your phone model, operating system, App version, language and country, to PostHog (PostHog Inc., EU data center). Purpose: improving the App. Events never contain your weight, your exact fasting times or any health data. Turn them off in Settings > What is sent (crash reports and statistics are switched off together).

c) Purchases
Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. To show offers and check your Premium status, the App uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States), which receives an anonymous user ID, your purchase history (product, dates, renewal status), your device type, App version and country. On iOS, the App also passes Apple's attribution token (AdServices) to RevenueCat to know whether an install came from an Apple Search Ads campaign; this uses no advertising identifier and no tracking. On Android, if you installed the App from one of our ad links (for example an ad on Google), the App reads once, from Google Play, the campaign name we wrote in that link (for example "google-ads / paid / fasting-us-test1"; "google-play / organic" when there was no ad link) and sends it to RevenueCat and with the usage statistics below. It is a label of our own link, not an identifier. It follows the usage statistics switch (Settings > What is sent): when they are off, the App does not read it. Purpose: measuring our own ads.

d) Ad measurement (Android, only if you came from one of our ads)
if you came from one of our ads, the App tells our measurement partner Tenjin (Tenjin, Inc., tenjin.com) that it was installed, with your device's advertising identifier, your IP address and the device model, so we know which ad brought you and whether you later started a trial. Tenjin passes this to the ad network that showed the ad (TikTok, Meta, Google). In the EU, the UK and Switzerland this happens only if you allow it; everywhere you can turn it off at any time in Settings > What is sent. Purpose: measuring our own ads. Retention: as long as needed to measure the campaigns, up to 2 years.

e) Ideas & feedback board — only what you post
If you post or vote on the Ideas & feedback board, the App sends the text you write, your votes, an anonymous random device identifier, the App version, your platform and language, and your email address only if you choose to enter it (it is never shown publicly and only used to reply to you). Requests can be shown to other users of the App. They are stored with Supabase (Supabase, Inc.), and their text may be translated into other languages by an AI service (OpenAI) so other users can read them: a server function of ours, hosted with Supabase, sends only the text of the request to the AI service, has it translated and keeps the translation on the board. The AI service receives nothing else from you. Do not include personal or health information in your posts.

The App shows no ads, uses no advertising identifier except for the ad measurement described above (Android, only if you came from one of our ads), and does not sell your personal data.


5. DEVICE PERMISSIONS

- Notifications: for the reminders you turn on (start of your fast, eve of a fasting day) and the "goal reached" notification, all scheduled on your phone.
- Health Connect / Apple Health (weight, read only): section 3.
- Internet: for the services in section 4.


6. LEGAL BASES (GDPR)

- Data stored on your device: not processed by us.
- Crash reports and usage statistics: our legitimate interest in fixing bugs and improving the App; you can turn them off at any time in the App or object by writing to us.
- Ad measurement (section 4d): your consent in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, our legitimate interest in measuring our own ads elsewhere; you can turn it off at any time in the App.
- Purchases: performance of the contract (your subscription or purchase).
- Ideas & feedback board: your consent when you choose to post or vote, and our legitimate interest in improving the App.

Data sent to our providers may be processed outside the European Union (in particular in the United States), under the safeguards those providers offer (such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).


7. RETENTION AND YOUR RIGHTS

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or the App. Crash reports are kept up to 90 days, usage statistics according to our provider's retention settings, purchase records as long as required for your purchases and by law, board posts until you ask us to delete them. You can ask to access or delete the data described in section 4, or object to its processing, by writing to hey@appkickstarter.com (tell us roughly when you used the App and on which device, as there is no account). You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France: the CNIL).


8. CHILDREN

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires) and we do not knowingly collect data from children.


9. NOT MEDICAL ADVICE

The App is a timer and tracking tool for intermittent fasting. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. The fasting phases it describes are general information that varies between people. Intermittent fasting is not suitable for everyone: talk to a doctor before fasting if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes, an eating disorder or any medical condition, or take medication.


10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy, for example when the App starts sending something new. The date at the top shows the latest version, and important changes are announced in the App.
