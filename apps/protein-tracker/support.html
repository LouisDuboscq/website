Simple Protein Tracker — Support

Simple Protein Tracker (package com.appkickstarter.proteintracker) is developed by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: hey@appkickstarter.com.

Premium

Premium is billed by Google Play (or the App Store) and removes the ads. To cancel a subscription, open your store subscriptions (Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions; iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions). To get Premium back on a new phone, tap "Restore purchases" in the app.

Your data

Your protein entries, foods and goal stay on your phone; there is no account. Deleting the app deletes them. Details: Privacy policy.
