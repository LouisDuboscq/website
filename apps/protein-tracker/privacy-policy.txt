PRIVACY POLICY — SIMPLE PROTEIN TRACKER

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app "Simple Protein Tracker" (Google Play listing "Simple Protein Tracker", French listing "Suivi Simple des Protéines", package name com.appkickstarter.proteintracker), called "the App" below.

The App is developed and published by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer (developer name on Google Play: "Louis Duboscq"; website: appkickstarter.com). Louis Duboscq is the data controller for the data described in this policy.

Contact: hey@appkickstarter.com


1. SUMMARY

- What you log (protein intake, foods, goals) stays on your phone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
- There is no account.
- The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Premium purchases go through Google Play and RevenueCat (section 3).
- Anonymous usage statistics are on by default; you can turn them off at any time in Settings > What is sent (section 3 d).


2. DATA STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The following data is stored on your phone only. We cannot access it:

- Your protein entries and foods, with their dates and amounts
- Your daily goal, settings, theme and history
- The data shown in the App's home screen widgets

Deleting the App deletes all of this data.


3. DATA THAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE

a) Advertising
The free version of the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To request, show and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends to Google your device's advertising ID (or app set ID), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, your phone model, operating system, language and App version, and ad interactions (which ads were shown and clicked). Ad requests never contain what you log in the App. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads are personalized only with your consent. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

b) Purchases
Premium purchases are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details. To check your Premium status, the App uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (product, dates, renewal status). Purpose: unlocking Premium and restoring purchases. Buying Premium removes the ads.

c) Ratings
If you choose to rate the App, the rating window is shown by Google Play; we do not receive your rating through the App.

d) Usage statistics — anonymous, on by default, you can turn them off
Usage statistics are on by default, without a question at launch: you can turn them off at any time in Settings > What is sent. Unless you turn them off, the App sends to PostHog (PostHog Inc., EU cloud, servers in the European Union): the steps you go through (for example the welcome pages seen, the Premium screen viewed or closed and the plan chosen, "entry added" with the way it was added: form, preset or quick action — never a food name, an amount or your goal), when the App is opened or put in the background, a random identifier created on your phone (replaced after 13 months at most), the App version, your phone model and operating system, language and time zone, the type of network (Wi-Fi or mobile, and the carrier name). PostHog also works out your country from your internet address when the statistics arrive; only the country is kept, not the address, region or city. There is no account, no profile and no screen recording. Purpose: understanding which features and screens help, to improve the App. Retention: up to 1 year.

We do not use crash reporting services, we do not sell your data and we do not share it with data brokers.


4. DEVICE PERMISSIONS

- Internet: only for ads, purchases and usage statistics (section 3). Advertising ID: only for ads (section 3 a).


5. LEGAL BASES (GDPR)

- Data stored on your device: not processed by us.
- Ads: your consent for personalized ads and for storing or accessing information on your device; Google's own legal bases for non-personalized ads.
- Purchases: performance of the contract (your purchase or subscription).
- Usage statistics: anonymous measurement of how the App is used, limited to what is needed to improve it, which the French data protection authority allows without prior consent (CNIL guidelines on audience measurement: anonymous statistics for this App only, no profile, identifier replaced after 13 months at most, kept up to 1 year); legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the App (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object at any time by turning them off in the App.

Data sent to our providers may be processed outside the European Union (in particular in the United States), under the safeguards those providers offer (such as Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).


6. RETENTION AND YOUR RIGHTS

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or the App. Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for your purchases and by law. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own policies. Usage statistics are kept up to 1 year.

You can ask questions or exercise your rights (access, deletion, objection) by writing to hey@appkickstarter.com. As there is no account, we only hold an anonymous purchase identifier. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France: the CNIL).


7. CHILDREN

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires) and we do not knowingly collect data from children.


8. NOT MEDICAL ADVICE

The App is a personal tracking tool. It does not provide medical or nutritional advice. Consult a healthcare professional for health decisions.


9. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy, for example when the App starts sending something new. The date at the top shows the latest version.
