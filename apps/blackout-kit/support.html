Blackout Kit: Emergency Prep — Support

Blackout Kit: Emergency Prep (iPhone and Android, package com.appkickstarter.blackoutkit) is developed by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: hey@appkickstarter.com, or the Ideas board in the app.

Your kit

Your household and your checklist stay on your phone; there is no account and the app works offline. Deleting the app deletes them. Usage statistics can be turned off in Settings > Privacy > "What is sent". Details: Privacy policy.

Quantities

Quantities follow the French government guide « Tous responsables » (SGDSN, 2025). Blackout Kit is an independent app, not affiliated with any government, and does not replace the instructions of your local authorities.
