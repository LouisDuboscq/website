Messages do not show up

Ghost only sees what your messaging apps put in their notifications. Check that:

  • notification access is on for Ghost (Android settings > Notifications > Device & app notifications);
  • the messaging app is enabled in Ghost's settings;
  • the messaging app shows notifications with their content (a muted chat or a hidden preview sends nothing to read);
  • battery optimization does not stop Ghost (Android settings > Apps > Ghost > Battery > Unrestricted).

Messages received before Ghost was installed cannot be recovered: they were never in a notification Ghost saw.

Premium

Ghost Premium (yearly subscription or lifetime purchase) is billed by Google Play. To cancel a subscription, open Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. To get Premium back on a new phone, sign in with the same Google account and tap "Restore purchases" on the Premium screen.

Your data

Your messages stay on your phone; there is no account and they are excluded from Google backups. Deleting the app deletes them. To move them to a new phone, use Settings > Backup & new phone (Premium: a file encrypted with your password). Details: Privacy policy.
