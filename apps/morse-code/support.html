Morse Code — Support

Morse Code (App Store: "Morse Code Translator & games"; Google Play: "Morse Code: Learn & Translate") is developed by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: hey@appkickstarter.com.

Progress from the previous version

When you update from version 1, your levels and stars (Latin and Cyrillic) are kept automatically the first time you open the app. If something is missing, write to us with your phone model.

Subscription

Premium is billed by the App Store or Google Play. To cancel, open your store subscriptions (iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions; Android: Google Play > Payments & subscriptions). To get Premium back on a new phone, tap "Restore purchases" in the app (a parent may need to answer the parental question).

Your data

Your progress and everything you type stay on your phone; there is no account. Deleting the app deletes them. Details: Privacy policy.
