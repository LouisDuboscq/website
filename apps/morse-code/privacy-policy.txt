PRIVACY POLICY — MORSE CODE

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile app "Morse Code" (App Store name "Morse Code Translator & games", bundle ID com.lduboscq.Morse-Code; Google Play listing "Morse Code: Learn & Translate", package name com.appkickstarter.morsecode), called "the App" below.

The App is developed and published by Louis Duboscq, an independent developer (developer name on Google Play: "Louis Duboscq"; website: appkickstarter.com). Louis Duboscq is the data controller for the data described in this policy.

Contact: hey@appkickstarter.com


1. SUMMARY

- The App is made for learners of all ages, including children. It is in the Kids category of the App Store.
- Your progress, settings and everything you type or translate stay on your phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.
- There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no advertising ID, no tracking across apps or websites and no sale of data.
- The App only sends the anonymous data listed in section 3: crash reports and anonymous usage statistics (which you can turn off), and purchases.
- Purchases, links that leave the App and sharing are behind a parental gate (a question for adults).


2. DATA STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The following data is stored on your phone only. It never leaves your device, we cannot access it, and it is not backed up to our servers:

- Your learning progress: lessons and levels passed, stars, practice results, speed records and daily streak (including progress kept from earlier versions of the App).
- Your settings: alphabet (Latin or Cyrillic), speed, tone, volume, light and vibration options, reminders.
- Everything you type, translate or key in Morse code.

Audio files you create with the Export feature are generated on your phone. They are only shared when you choose to do so, through the sharing app you pick.

Deleting the App deletes all of this data.


3. DATA THAT LEAVES YOUR DEVICE

a) Crash reports
When the App crashes or hits an error, it sends anonymous technical data to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., sentry.io), our crash reporting provider: the error and where it happened, the phone's operating system and App version, and the names of the screens opened just before the problem (never what you typed). It contains nothing that identifies you or your phone. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > "Anonymous statistics and crash reports". Purpose: finding and fixing bugs. Retention: up to 90 days.

b) Anonymous usage statistics
The App sends to PostHog (PostHog Inc., posthog.com, servers in the European Union) which features are used, for example that a lesson was finished, a practice mode or a tool was opened, or the Premium screen was shown — never what you typed or translated. Each event comes with a random identifier that is replaced by a new one every 30 days, the App version, the operating system, the language and the screen size. The App never sends the phone model, its name or identifiers, the network or carrier, the time zone or the internet address. PostHog may work out the country from the internet address when the statistics arrive; only the country is kept, not the address, region or city. There are no user profiles, no screen recording, and statistics are never linked to a person. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > "Anonymous statistics and crash reports" (the same switch as crash reports). Purpose: understanding which lessons and tools help, to improve the App. Retention: up to 1 year.

c) Purchases
Subscriptions and purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play; we never see payment details. A parental gate is shown before any purchase or restore. To check Premium status, the App uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase history (product, dates, renewal status). Purpose: unlocking Premium and restoring purchases.

We do not use any advertising SDK, we do not track you across apps or websites, we do not ask for or collect a name, email, photo, location or contact, and we do not sell or share personal data with data brokers or advertisers.


4. DEVICE PERMISSIONS AND FEATURES

- Flashlight and vibration: to play Morse code with the light and vibration when you ask. Nothing is recorded or sent.
- Notifications: for the practice reminder you turn on in Settings. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no data is sent.
- Internet: only for the cases in section 3.

The App does not use the camera, the microphone, photos, contacts or location.


5. CHILDREN

The App is designed to be safe for children, in line with the App Store Kids category rules and with laws protecting children's privacy (such as COPPA in the United States and the GDPR in Europe):

- No advertising and no third-party analytics that identify a child or a device: the statistics in section 3 b) are anonymous and contain nothing that describes the child or the phone.
- No account, no chat, no free text sent anywhere and no way to contact other users.
- Purchases, restoring purchases, links to websites or the stores, and sharing are protected by a parental gate.
- A parent can turn off crash reports and statistics in Settings at any time.

If you are a parent and have a question about your child's use of the App, write to hey@appkickstarter.com.


6. LEGAL BASES (GDPR)

- Data stored on your device: not processed by us.
- Crash reports and anonymous usage statistics: our legitimate interest in keeping the App working and improving it, with data minimised so it cannot identify anyone, and a switch to turn it off at any time.
- Purchases: performance of the contract (your subscription or purchase).

Data sent to our providers may be processed outside the European Union (in particular in the United States), under the safeguards those providers offer (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).


7. RETENTION AND YOUR RIGHTS

Data on your device stays there until you delete it or the App. Crash reports are kept up to 90 days, usage statistics up to 1 year.

You can ask to access or delete the data described in section 3, or object to its processing, by writing to hey@appkickstarter.com. As there is no account and only anonymous identifiers, we usually cannot link data to a person; turning off the switch in Settings stops any new data. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France: the CNIL).


8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy, for example when the App starts sending something new. The date at the top shows the latest version, and important changes are announced in the App.
